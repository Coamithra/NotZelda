Legends of Amara — Official Trailer

What happens when you ask an AI to make a game trailer with zero guidance? This.

Legends of Amara is a browser-based multiplayer MUD rendered as a Zelda-style top-down adventure. Explore a hand-crafted overworld, delve into procedurally generated dungeons, battle monsters, chat with AI-powered NPCs, and team up with friends — all from your browser.

Features:
- Multiplayer real-time exploration with server-authoritative netcode
- Procedurally generated dungeons with locked doors, keys, dark rooms, and boss fights
- AI-powered NPCs that remember your conversations and react to your behavior
- Pixel-art style visuals rendered entirely in HTML5 Canvas
- Original music for each area (overworld, dungeons, boss encounters)
- Revival system — revive fallen allies or use a Spirit Jar for a second chance
- No downloads, no installs — just open a browser and play

This entire trailer — title sequence, game scene rendering, transitions, music sync — was generated by Claude (Anthropic's AI) using Python, OpenCV, and Pillow. No human editing, no video software. The sprites and rooms are rendered from the actual game data files. Claude did make up a URL that doesn't exist though. Classic AI move.

Built with Python, websockets, and questionable life choices.
